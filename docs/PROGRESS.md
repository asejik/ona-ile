# Ọ̀nà Ilé: Progress

## Current state (2026-11-20)

- **Prototype v0.1** in `baba-keke/` is a single-file Three.js game with OSM zone data. It is published as an [artifact](https://claude.ai/artifact/LVmMr8ENW5UvSUw12b2p9V). It ran well on a real phone in mobile Chrome.
- **Planning is done:** PROMPT_PLAN (P00), IDEA_BRIEF (P01), PROJECT_PLAN, DATA_INVENTORY draft and CLAUDE.md (P02).
- **PRD:** https://claude.ai/artifact/DbR5D4bPuhmqcZtjaHE8A1. ACC-3 swipe-to-drive was added on 2026-11-07.
- **Repo:** `https://github.com/asejik/ona-ile` (private). The first commit was pushed on 2026-11-11.
- **M0 and M1 are done.** **M2 is done** (M2a, M2b, M2c): the prototype's world and ride loop are back, with HUD-1, CORE-4, CORE-5, ACC-3 swipe-to-drive, the controls setting and the save code. Owner confirmed swipe driving on the phone (M2 exit). **M3a is done:** junction data and signpost logic. **M3 is done** (M3a junction data, M3b plates and signposts, the street-name check, M3c maps). **Next:** M4 (damage, potholes, timed challenges and hills). Still open: owner checks 5 junctions on the [junction review page](https://claude.ai/artifact/XwGZ3xHTJiB27cqkgh7hov), remotely with its Street View, Google Maps, Bing and Mapillary links, and answers the name questions.
