Privacy notice

Draft, version 2026-11-draft. Not yet reviewed by a data protection officer. The game is not released.

Who is responsible

Ọ̀nà Ilé: The Last Delivery is made by an independent developer in Ilorin, Nigeria, who is the data controller. Name and privacy contact: [to be added before release].

The short version

  • You don't need an account. The game never asks for your name, email, phone number or location.
  • Your progress and settings are saved on your device only.
  • Crash reports are off unless you turn them on, and only adults (18 or older) are asked.

What stays on your device

Your game progress, your settings, your diagnostics choice (with the date you made it), and a copy of a small settings file from our server. Clearing the site's data or uninstalling the app removes them.

What leaves your device

  • Loading the game. Like any website, the server that sends you the game files sees your IP address and browser type. The game is hosted by Cloudflare. This is needed to deliver the game and to protect it from abuse.
  • Crash reports, only if you opt in. If the game hits an error, it sends the error message, where in the game's code it happened, the game version, and your browser and operating system version. It does not send your name, an account, a user ID, cookies or the page address. Reports go to Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.), stored in the European Union, which is outside Nigeria. We ask Sentry not to store IP addresses.

We use this only to find and fix crashes. We don't sell data or show adverts.

Why we are allowed to do this

  • Delivering the game to you: it is necessary to provide what you asked for.
  • Crash reports: your consent, which also covers sending them outside Nigeria.

Your choices and rights

  • You can turn crash reports on or off at any time in the game's settings. Saying no changes nothing else in the game.
  • Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act you can ask what data we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it. Because crash reports carry no name or ID, we usually can't tell which reports are yours.
  • You can complain to us using the contact above, and to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission at ndpc.gov.ng.

Under 18

The game is not designed for children. Players who do not confirm they are 18 or older are never asked to send crash reports, and none are sent.
