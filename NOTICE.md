# Notices

## Map data: OpenStreetMap

Road, building and place data in `data/osm/` and the generated zone files
(`baba-keke/zone1.js` and `public/zones/*.json`) are derived from
OpenStreetMap.

Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database
License (ODbL) 1.0: https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright

The game shows this credit on its title and credits screens.

Share-alike: the derived zone data in `public/zones/`, and the tool that makes
it (`tools/build_zone.py`, with `content/zones/`), are offered under the ODbL
1.0.
